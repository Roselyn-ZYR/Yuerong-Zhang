About

I am an artist, architect and researcher based in Weimar, Germany. I work with interactive installations, ceramics and robotic fabrication. My current research brings Nüshu and feminist histories into dialogue with material and computational processes.

I studied architecture at Chang'an University and worked in robotic construction at CCCC from 2019 to 2023. I am now pursuing an M.Sc. in MediaArchitecture at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar.
