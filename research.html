Nüshu & feminist histories

I explore how women's writing carries memory, experience and knowledge. My work brings Nüshu into dialogue with Chinese and Western feminist histories through ceramics, light, digital archives and performance.

Robotic material systems

I work between digital models and physical processes, studying how feedback and material variation shape fabrication. My experience includes concrete 3D printing and robotic assembly with non-standard glass elements.
